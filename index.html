Am I OK to spend? Know your safe amount for today in two seconds.

What Okayo does

Okayo looks at your balance, upcoming bills, savings plan and emergency reserve, then spreads what's left across the days until payday. The result is one calm number: how much you can safely spend today. Overspend one day and Okayo quietly adjusts the rest of the period — no guilt, no lectures.

Frequently asked questions

How is my daily amount calculated?
Safe pool = available balance − unpaid upcoming bills − planned savings − emergency reserve. Daily safe-to-spend = safe pool ÷ days until payday. Tap "How is this calculated?" on the home screen to see your own numbers.

Do I need to connect my bank?
No. You enter transactions manually, import them with one tap, or scan receipts with Okayo+.

What does Okayo+ include?
The Smart Money Coach and AI Receipt Scan. Everything else — the safe-to-spend engine, bills, goals, budgets, calendar, widgets and Live Activity — is free. Okayo+ is $7.99/month or $59.99/year with a 7-day free trial.

How do I cancel or restore a subscription?
Cancel in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions. To restore on a new device, open Okayo › Profile › Okayo+ › Restore Purchases.

Where is my data stored?
On your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy for details.
