Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

Okayo: Am I OK to Spend? ("Okayo", "the app") tells you how much you can safely spend today. It is built so your financial data never leaves your iPhone. This policy explains exactly what the app does with information.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Everything you enter stays on your device. Okayo has no servers that receive your data.
  • We never collect, sell or share your personal or financial information.

1. Data stored on your device

Your balance, income, transactions, bills, savings goals, budgets, challenges and preferences are saved locally with Apple's SwiftData. A small summary (today's safe-to-spend numbers) is shared with Okayo's own widgets and Live Activity through an App Group container on the same device. Nothing is uploaded.

2. Network use

Okayo works fully offline and does not send your data anywhere. Links you choose to open, such as this policy, open in your browser.

3. Notifications

Bill reminders and alerts are scheduled locally on your iPhone. No push notification server is involved.

4. Purchases

If Okayo offers optional in-app purchases, they are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple ID or payment information.

5. Children

Okayo is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

6. Your choices and deletion

You can edit or delete any entry in the app, reset all data from Settings inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything it stored. Because we keep no copy of your data, there is nothing further for us to delete.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

8. Contact

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/pvhung1403/okayo-app/issues.
